Straight answers

Trust likes
the fine print.

Who owns the website and domain?

You own your domain and the content we create for you. Northstar manages the infrastructure while we work together. If you leave, we provide a clean export and a reasonable transition window.

What happens if we cancel?

There is no lock-in beyond the minimum term. We give 30 days notice, export your site and lead data, and document the handoff. Your domain stays yours.

How does the AI behave?

It only answers from an approved knowledge base, shows its limits, and hands off to a person when a question is unclear or sensitive. We review its performance monthly.

What do you need from us?

A point person, access to your existing website and CRM, service details, photos or brand assets, and timely feedback. Content delays are the most common cause of a slower launch.

Do you guarantee SEO rankings?

No. We build a fast, accessible, technically sound foundation and track meaningful actions. Rankings depend on competition, location, content, and many factors outside a website.
